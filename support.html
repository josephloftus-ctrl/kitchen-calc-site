Kitchen Calc

Questions, bugs, or a recipe that won't parse? Email josephloftus@outlook.com and include the ingredient lines if it's a scaling problem.

How do I write ingredients?

One per line, as quantity, unit, name: 2 1/2 cups AP flour, 500 g butter, 3 eggs. Fractions like ½ and mixed numbers like 2 1/2 work. Use a capital T for tablespoon and lowercase t for teaspoon.

How does food cost work?

Enter what you paid for a pack (size, unit, price) and how much the recipe used. Units convert automatically within weight or within volume; weight-to-volume needs the same kind on both sides.
