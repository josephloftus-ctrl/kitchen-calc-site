Privacy policy

Kitchen Calc · effective October 3, 2026

Kitchen Calc does not collect, store, or transmit any personal data. It has no accounts, no analytics, no advertising, and makes no network requests of its own.

Recipes and costings you save stay on your device, in the app's own storage. Deleting the app deletes them.

Purchases of Kitchen Calc Pro are processed by Apple through the App Store. The developer never sees your payment details or Apple ID.

If this policy ever changes, the new version will be posted on this page before the change ships.
